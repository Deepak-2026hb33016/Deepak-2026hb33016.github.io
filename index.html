                                    Problem
                                    Ad-hoc effort estimation during regional intake caused frequent mid-sprint scope escalations when custom designs exceeded standard CMS capabilities.
                                

                                

                                    Constraint
                                    Estimates needed to be transparent, repeatable across non-technical stakeholders, and exportable into standardized SOW formats.
                                

                                

                                    Approach
                                    Built a component-mapping checklist paired with calibrated hour multipliers for authoring, design feedback cycles, and compliance QC.
                                

                                

                                    Measurable Result
                                    Reduced intake scoping turnaround from ~2 days to under 15 minutes and cut mid-sprint change requests by ~40%.
                                

                            

                        

                    

                


                

                    
                    

                        

                            

                                

                                    Problem
                                    Manual side-by-side proofreading of regulated copy across PDF annotations, Word manuscripts, and staged web pages was slow and fatigue-prone.
                                

                                

                                    Constraint
                                    Enterprise information security policies strictly blocked third-party browser extensions and external SaaS diff utilities.
                                

                                

                                    Approach
                                    Engineered an air-gapped client-side LCS diff engine supporting local file parsing, inline/mismatches-only views, and exportable discrepancy logs.
                                

                                

                                    Measurable Result
                                    Cut pre-publish copy verification cycles from ~4.5 hours to ~35 minutes per multi-market release with zero external data exposure.
                                

                            

                        

                    

                


                

                    
                    

                        

                            

                                

                                    Problem
                                    Silent asset 404s, slow clientlib hydration, and transient endpoint timeouts often went unnoticed until reported by regional brand teams.
                                

                                

                                    Constraint
                                    Needed lightweight automated audits across multi-locale page trees without adding heavy third-party APM overhead.
                                

                                

                                    Approach
                                    Developed scheduled DOM/HTTP crawlers that capture status codes, response timings, and structural anomalies into actionable alerts.
                                

                                

                                    Measurable Result
                                    Reclaimed ~6+ hours/week of manual post-deployment spot checks and surfaced transient outages within minutes of occurrence.
                                

                            

                        

                    

                


                

                    
                    

                        

                            

                                

                                    Problem
                                    Inconsistent dialog configurations and fragmented DAM naming conventions slowed multi-market page assembly and localization.
                                

                                

                                    Constraint
                                    Had to preserve creative flexibility for regional campaigns while enforcing global accessibility and brand guardrails.
                                

                                

                                    Approach
                                    Mapped AS-IS bottlenecks, standardized reusable component contracts, and embedded pre-publish validation gates into authoring workflows.
                                

                                

                                    Measurable Result
                                    Improved first-pass QA approval rates and standardized multi-market launch playbooks across distributed production squads.
